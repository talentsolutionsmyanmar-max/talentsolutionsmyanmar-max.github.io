Negotiating Contract Renewals

CORE INSIGHT

A contract renewal is not your employer doing you a favor. It is a business decision: replacing you costs them 3-6 months of productivity, recruitment fees (often 1-2 months of your salary paid to an agency), and training time. When your employer renews your contract, they have already decided you are worth keeping. That is leverage, and most Myanmar professionals never use it.

The mistake is treating renewal as a yes-or-no question -- 'Will they keep me?' The real question is: 'Under what terms will I continue?' Every element is negotiable: salary, title, benefits, leave days, training budget, remote work flexibility, even contract length. The moment between your employer deciding to renew and you signing is when you have maximum power. Once you sign, that power disappears for another 12 months.

Here is what works versus what most people do. Most Myanmar professionals wait passively, receive the renewal letter, and sign within 24 hours out of gratitude or fear. Professionals who advance treat renewal as a scheduled negotiation. They prepare a one-page summary of their contributions, they know the current market rate for their role, and they request a meeting specifically to discuss terms before signing anything.

THE MYANMAR REALITY

In Myanmar, contract structures vary dramatically by employer type. Local companies often use rolling verbal agreements with no formal renewal point, which means you must create your own negotiation moment -- typically at your annual review or the start of the fiscal year. NGOs and INGOs usually have fixed-term contracts of 6-12 months with formal renewal processes, and salary scales are more rigid but benefits like training allowances or hardship pay are negotiable. MNCs like Golden Beverage Co., Lotus Consumer Goods, or Jade Ride tend to have annual reviews tied to performance ratings, and your renewal terms often depend on your rating score.

Culturally, many Myanmar professionals feel that asking for more during renewal shows ingratitude. Senior managers may even say things like 'You should be thankful we are renewing in this economy.' This is a negotiation tactic, not a fact. In 2026, with AI tools automating junior tasks and companies restructuring, professionals who have proven they can deliver results that AI cannot replicate have more leverage than they realize -- not less.

COMMON MISTAKE

The most damaging mistake is signing a renewal contract without reading the full terms because you feel pressured by time or gratitude. Many Myanmar professionals discover too late that their new contract includes changed terms -- a shifted probation period, removed overtime pay, a non-compete clause that did not exist before, or a changed termination notice period. One common tactic by employers is to quietly reduce benefits while keeping the salary the same, so the total compensation actually decreases. Always compare the new contract line-by-line against your current one before signing. You are legally entitled to take at least a few days to review.

ACTION STEPS

  1. Thirty days before your contract expires, send a professional email to your manager: 'I would like to schedule a meeting to discuss my contract renewal and review my contributions from this period.' This frames renewal as a discussion, not a rubber stamp.
  1. Prepare a one-page achievement summary. List 3-5 specific results: revenue you generated, problems you solved, responsibilities you absorbed. Use numbers wherever possible. Example: 'Managed client onboarding for 15 new accounts after team downsized from 4 to 2 people.'
  1. Check your current market value. Go to MyJobs.com.mm and JobNet.com.mm, search your exact job title filtered to Yangon, and note the salary range in at least 5 active postings. Screenshot them.
  1. Read the new contract word-by-word. Compare every clause to your current contract using a simple two-column table. Flag anything that changed. Do not sign on the same day you receive it.
  1. In your renewal meeting, present your achievements first, then state your specific request: 'Based on my expanded responsibilities and current market rates, I am requesting a salary adjustment to [amount].' Then stop talking and let them respond.
