This assessment covers everything you have learned in the Sales and Revenue Development program. It is not a test you pass or fail — it is a mirror that shows you where you are strong and where you need more practice. Honest self-assessment is the foundation of growth.

The assessment evaluates 6 core sales competencies. For each one, rate yourself honestly from 1 (I have not started developing this skill) to 5 (I can teach this skill to others).

Competency 1: Sales Mindset. Do you see sales as a career you are proud of? Can you explain the value of your product without reading from a script? When you hear "no," do you learn from it or avoid the next call? A strong sales mindset means you believe in what you sell and you see rejection as information, not failure.

Competency 2: Customer Understanding. Can you describe your ideal customer with specific details? Do you know their top 3 frustrations before you meet them? Can you explain how your product solves their specific problem? Customer understanding means you have done your research before the conversation starts.

Competency 3: Communication. Can you adapt your communication style to different channels — Viber for quick updates, phone for negotiations, in-person for relationship building? Do you follow up within 24 hours of every meeting? Can you explain complex ideas in simple language? Communication competency means you are clear, timely, and appropriate for each context.

Competency 4: Pipeline Management. Do you track every prospect in a system (notebook, spreadsheet, or CRM)? Can you calculate how many prospects you need to hit your monthly target? Do you update your pipeline daily? Pipeline management means you know exactly where every potential deal stands, every day.

Competency 5: Objection Handling and Negotiation. When a prospect says "too expensive," do you have a prepared response? Can you negotiate terms beyond just price — delivery, volume, payment terms? Do you stay calm under pressure? This competency means you can navigate difficult conversations with confidence and respect.

Competency 6: Trust Building. Do your existing customers refer new business to you? When you make a promise, do you keep it every single time? Can you name 3 personal details about your top 5 clients? Trust building means you have invested in relationships that go beyond transactions.

Scoring: 25-30: You are ready for a senior sales role. Focus on mentoring others and expanding your strategic skills. 18-24: You have solid foundations. Identify your weakest competency and spend the next 30 days practicing it intentionally. 12-17: You are building. Review the modules for your lowest-scoring competencies and create a 90-day practice plan. Below 12: You are at the beginning of an exciting journey. Start with Module 1 (Sales Mindset) and work through each module in order.

Remember: this assessment is a starting point, not a judgment. The greatest salespeople in Myanmar were all beginners once. What separates them from everyone else is that they kept learning, kept practicing, and never stopped improving.
