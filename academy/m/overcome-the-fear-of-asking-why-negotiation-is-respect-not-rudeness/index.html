Overcome the Fear of Asking — Why Negotiation Is Respect, Not Rudeness

တောင်းဆိုရန် ကြောက်ရွံ့မှုကို ကျော်လွှားပါ — ညှိနှိုင်းဆွေးနွေးခြင်းသည် ရိုင်းစိုင်းမှုမဟုတ်ဘဲ လေးစားမှုဖြစ်ကြောင်း သိရှိနိုင်ပါသည်

The storyCard 1 of 12
The Story

Ko Zaw Htet is 28 years old and received a job offer from a food manufacturing company in South Dagon, Yangon. The offer was 400,000 kyats per month. Ko Zaw Htet knew from his research that the market rate for his quality control experience was 500,000 to 550,000 kyats. He wrote a counter-offer message. He stared at it on his phone for two hours. Then he deleted it and accepted 400,000. His reasoning: 'If I negotiate, they might think I am difficult and cancel the offer. In this economy, I cannot risk losing a sure thing.' Six months later, Ko Zaw Htet is struggling to cover rent in Thaketa after two rounds of landlord increases driven by inflation. He works overtime without extra pay because he feels he cannot ask — he already feels he should be grateful they hired him. The irony is devastating: the HR manager later told a mutual friend they had budget approved up to 520,000 for that role. They expected Ko Zaw Htet to negotiate. He never did.

The common mistakeCard 3 of 12
The Common Trap

The most common mistake is catastrophizing the negotiation: believing that any pushback will cause the employer to withdraw the offer entirely. The cognitive distortion is catastrophic thinking — your brain jumps to the worst possible outcome and treats it as the most likely outcome. The cultural root cause is Myanmar's deep hierarchy respect and face-saving norm: questioning an authority figure's first offer feels like challenging their judgment, which risks relationship damage. The devastating consequence is a pattern of chronic under-earning across your entire career. Each accepted lowball offer becomes the baseline for the next job. After ten years, workers who never negotiate earn 30-50% less cumulatively than peers with identical skills who simply asked.

The teachingCard 4 of 12
The truth about what happens when you negotiate

Here is a fact that contradicts what most Myanmar workers believe: according to multiple HR industry surveys, fewer than 5% of employers withdraw job offers because a candidate negotiated respectfully. Most employers — including major Myanmar employers like a major conglomerate and an established Myanmar bank — build negotiation room into their initial offers. They expect you to counter. When you accept the first number without question, you do not earn gratitude — you signal that you either do not know your value or lack the confidence to advocate for yourself. Neither impression helps your career. Nassim Taleb teaches asymmetric thinking: what is the downside of negotiating respectfully? At worst, they say no and you accept the original offer. What is the upside? Potentially hundreds of thousands of kyats per month for years. The risk is tiny. The reward is enormous. This is an asymmetric opportunity — and refusing to take it is the truly risky choice, because you guarantee the loss. Celebrate the process of asking, not just the result. Even a 'no' means you practiced a skill that compounds over your career.

The teachingCard 5 of 12
Negotiating within Myanmar's face-saving culture without damaging relationships

The fear of negotiating in Myanmar is rooted in a real cultural value: maintaining harmony and showing respect for authority. This value is not wrong — it is important. But it has been misapplied to salary discussions. There is a crucial difference between challenging someone's authority and professionally discussing compensation. The key is framing. In Myanmar workplaces, effective negotiation follows three cultural rules. First, always express genuine gratitude before any counter-proposal. This honors the relationship. Second, use indirect phrasing: 'I was hoping we might discuss' rather than 'I want more.' Third, reference external factors — market data, inflation, commute costs — rather than personal desire. This allows the employer to adjust without losing face, because the change is driven by market reality, not your demand. At local Myanmar companies where relationships drive advancement, this approach actually builds respect. At MNCs like a major multinational based in Yangon or Total Myanmar Energy, HR professionals are specifically trained to expect negotiation and may privately question the judgment of candidates who do not negotiate at all.

Do thisCard 6 of 12
Action 1

Open the Notes app on your phone. Write down every fear you have about negotiating salary. Be honest and specific. For each fear, write next to it: 'What is the actual evidence this has happened to me or someone I know personally?' You will likely discover that most fears are imagined catastrophes, not documented events. This exercise from cognitive behavioral practice separates genuine risk from distortion. Save this note and title it 'My Negotiation Fears vs Reality.'

Do thisCard 7 of 12
Action 2

Open your phone browser and search 'do employers withdraw offers if you negotiate' in English or Myanmar. Read at least three articles or forum discussions. You will find overwhelming evidence from HR professionals globally and in Myanmar that reasonable negotiation almost never results in offer withdrawal. Screenshot the most convincing evidence and save it in your Market Value folder. This evidence becomes your courage anchor when fear strikes during real negotiations.

Do thisCard 8 of 12
Action 3

Open WhatsApp or Messenger. Draft a practice counter-offer message using this exact template and fill in your own details: 'Thank you very much for the offer of [amount]. I am genuinely excited about joining [company name] and contributing to [specific team or project]. Based on my research of current market rates for [role] with [X years] experience, and considering my specific skills in [your strength], I would like to respectfully discuss a salary of [your target amount]. I am flexible and very interested in finding an arrangement that works for both of us.' Do not send this to anyone yet. Save it as a draft. Read it aloud. Notice it is polite, data-referenced, and maintains face for both parties.

Do thisCard 9 of 12
Action 4

Find one trusted friend or family member on WhatsApp. Send them your draft counter-offer message and ask them to role-play the employer. Ask them to respond with a realistic reply — either accepting, meeting halfway, or holding firm. Practice your response to each scenario. This low-stakes rehearsal builds the neural pathways for real negotiation. Record the voice call if possible and listen back for tone, confidence, and clarity. Do this exercise at least twice with different people if possible.

Do thisCard 10 of 12
Action 5

Open YouTube on your phone. Search 'salary negotiation examples' and watch at least two videos under ten minutes. Watch specifically for tone of voice and body language — not aggressive, not pleading, but calm and factual. Note how professional negotiators reference data, not personal need. Even if videos are in English, the tone patterns transfer directly to Myanmar workplace conversations. Save your two favorite videos for review before any real negotiation.

Keep thisCard 11 of 12
Key Takeaway

Employers do not withdraw offers because you negotiate — they lose respect for you because you do not. The person who accepts everything without question signals that they do not know their own value.

ဇော်ထက်သည် တောင်ဒဂုံရှိ food manufacturing company တစ်ခုမှ Quality Control အလုပ်ကမ်းလှမ်းချက် ရရှိပါသည်။ လစာမှာ တစ်လ ၄၀၀,၀၀၀ ကျပ် ဖြစ်ပါသည်။ သူ၏အတွေ့အကြုံအတွက် ဈေးကွက်လစာသည် ၅၀၀,၀၀၀ မှ ၅၅၀,၀၀၀ ကျပ်အထိ ရှိနိုင်သည်ကို သူသိထားပါသည်။ သို့သော် counter-offer message ရေးပြီး နှစ်နာရီကြာ ကြည့်နေပြီးနောက် ဖျက်ပစ်ပါသည်။ အလုပ်ကမ်းလှမ်းချက် ပျက်သွားမည်ကို ကြောက်၍ ပထမကမ်းလှမ်းချက်ကို လက်ခံပါသည်။

ခြောက်လကြာပြီးနောက် သာကေတတွင် အိမ်ငှားခ တိုးလာသောကြောင့် သူ့လစာနှင့် မလုံလောက်တော့ပါ။ အချိန်ပိုလုပ်ရသော်လည်း မတောင်းရဲပါ။ နောက်ပိုင်းမှ သိရသည်မှာ HR manager သည် ထိုရာထူးအတွက် ၅၂၀,၀၀၀ ကျပ်အထိ budget ခွင့်ပြုထားပါသည်။ ကုမ္ပဏီသည် သူ counter-offer လုပ်မည်ဟု မျှော်လင့်ထားပါသည်။ သူမလုပ်ခဲ့ပါ။

အဓိကသင်ခန်းစာမှာ negotiation သည် ရိုင်းစိုင်းခြင်းမဟုတ်ပါ။ မိမိကျွမ်းကျင်မှုကို လေးစားခြင်းဖြစ်ပါသည်။ မြန်မာအလုပ်ခွင်တွင် အကြီးအကဲကို မေးမြန်းခြင်း၊ ပထမကမ်းလှမ်းချက်ကို ပြန်ဆွေးနွေးခြင်းသည် ဆက်ဆံရေးပျက်စေမည်ဟု လူအများ စိုးရိမ်ကြပါသည်။ ထိုစိုးရိမ်မှုသည် နားလည်နိုင်ပါသည်။ သို့သော် ယဉ်ကျေးသော salary negotiation သည် အလုပ်ရှင်ကို မထိခိုက်စေပါ။ HR survey များအရ ယဉ်ကျေးစွာညှိနှိုင်းသောကြောင့် offer ကို ပြန်ရုပ်သိမ်းသည့် အလုပ်ရှင်သည် ၅% ထက်နည်းပါသည်။

မြန်မာအလုပ်ခွင်တွင် negotiation လုပ်ရာတွင် နည်းလမ်းမှန် လိုအပ်ပါသည်။ ပထမဆုံး ကျေးဇူးတင်စကားပြောပါ။ ထို့နောက် market data၊ inflation၊ commute cost၊ role responsibility တို့ကို အကြောင်းပြု၍ ဆွေးနွေးပါ။ “ကျေးဇူးအများကြီးတင်ပါသည်။ ဤအခွင့်အလမ်းကို အလွန်စိတ်ဝင်စားပါသည်။ ဈေးကွက်လစာနှင့် ကျွန်ုပ်၏အတွေ့အကြုံကို အခြေခံ၍ compensation ကို ဆွေးနွေးနိုင်ပါမည်လား” ဟု ပြောနိုင်ပါသည်။

ပထမဆုံး “မေးရဲခြင်း” ကို လေ့ကျင့်ပါ။ ရလဒ်ကိုသာ အောင်မြင်မှုဟု မမြင်ပါနှင့်။ ယဉ်ကျေးစွာ မေးမြန်းနိုင်ခြင်းကိုယ်တိုင်သည် career skill ဖြစ်ပါသည်။ တစ်ခါ “မရပါ” ဟု ပြန်ဖြေခံရလျှင်ပင် နောက်အကြိမ်တွင် မိမိတန်ဖိုးကို ပိုမိုတည်ငြိမ်စွာ ပြောနိုင်လာပါသည်။
